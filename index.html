Read the passage and then choose the correct answers for (1 - 4).

Peter didn't have money to pay his bills, so he always owed money to the shopkeepers in his town. Most of them didn't ask for their money, but there was one who was not at all patient. Whenever this man saw Peter, he reminded him of the money that he had not yet paid him, and he argued about it in front of people, which made Peter feel very uncomfortable, as he did not want people to know that he was so poor.

One day, therefore, Peter decided to teach the shopkeeper a lesson. The next time that the man stopped him in the street and began to shame him publicly about his debt to him, Peter said, "Wait a minute. How much money do I owe you?" "You owe me exactly one hundred and twenty-two pounds." answered the shopkeeper. "Well," said Peter, "If I paid forty pounds this month, another forty next month, and forty more the month after, how much would I still owe you?" "You would then owe me two pounds, of course." answered the shopkeeper. "Well, aren't you ashamed of yourself," Peter said, "giving me all this trouble for only two pounds?"
